How it works

STEP 1

Upload

Report, code, data logs and figures in one ZIP.

STEP 2

Break down

Parts, equations and numbers, each traced to the report. Anything missing stays empty — never guessed.

STEP 3

Review

An instructor accepts it or returns it with notes.

STEP 4

Explore

Unfold the car, tap a part, and break it in a simulation.
